Why No Cloud? Absolute Data Sovereignty

Designed specifically for manufacturing plants, warehouses, and sensitive supply chains.

100% Local SQLite Storage

All materials, inventory codes, slip history, and device pairing tokens remain exclusively inside the SQLite database on your computer. No external third party ever receives your business records.

Direct End-to-End TLS

The Android scanner connects either directly via local Wi-Fi HTTPS or through a zero-config Cloudflare Tunnel. All requests require high-entropy bearer tokens with strict sliding-window rate limiting.

Offline Resilient Queue

If your warehouse Wi-Fi drops, workers can continue scanning barcodes. Scans are queued safely inside the phone's encrypted Room SQLite database and synchronized automatically once signal is restored.

Quick Start Guide

Get your desktop and mobile scanner running in less than 3 minutes.

01

Run Desktop Hub

Download and unzip IssueSlipManager.zip. Double click IssueSlipManager.exe. No installation needed.

02

Install Mobile APK

Download TTL-Scanner-1.0.0.apk on your Android phone and install it (allow install from this source if prompted).

03

Scan Pairing QR

On your desktop, click Connect Phone Scanner. Open TTL Scanner on your phone and scan the QR code to pair instantly!

04

Scan Barcodes

Tap [ ISSUE SLIP ] or [ PURCHASE ORDER ] on your phone. Scans appear live on your desktop screen instantly!

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setup and security.

Do I need an account or subscription to use TTL Scanner?

No. There are no subscriptions, accounts, or cloud logins. The software is completely self-hosted on your machine.

Can multiple phones connect to the same desktop computer?

Yes! Multiple workers can pair their phones simultaneously. Each phone receives a unique secure device token and scans are synchronized concurrently.

Does it work if there is no Internet connection at the plant?

Yes. If your computer and phones are connected to the same local Wi-Fi router, you can use the built-in Local Wi-Fi mode without any external Internet access.

How does Cloudflare Tunnel work without port forwarding?

The desktop software launches an official lightweight Cloudflare Tunnel subprocess. It creates an outbound TLS tunnel to Cloudflare edge nodes, allowing your phone to connect securely from anywhere without modifying firewall rules or forwarding router ports.
